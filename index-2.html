Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about buying, selling, and
investing in real estate

How do I know if I'm ready to buy a home?

You're ready to buy a home when you have stable income, a good credit score (typically 620+), a down payment
saved (usually 10-20% of the home price), and you plan to stay in the area for at least 3-5 years. Our team can help
assess your financial readiness and connect you with mortgage pre-approval services.

What's the best time of year to sell my home?

Spring and early summer (March through June) are traditionally the best times to sell, as buyers are more active and
homes show better with good weather. However, market conditions, local factors, and your personal timeline are
more important than the season. We can help you determine the optimal timing based on current market conditions in
your area.

How much should I offer on a house?

Your offer should be based on comparable sales in the area, the home's condition, current market conditions, and
your budget. In competitive markets, you might need to offer at or above asking price. Our agents will provide a
detailed comparative market analysis (CMA) to help you make a competitive yet reasonable offer.

What are closing costs and who pays them?

Closing costs typically range from 2-5% of the home's purchase price and include fees for loan origination, appraisal,
title insurance, attorney fees, and taxes. Both buyers and sellers have closing costs, though they vary by location and
can be negotiated. We'll provide a detailed breakdown of expected costs early in the process.

Should I get a home inspection?

Yes, absolutely! A professional home inspection typically costs $300-600 but can save you thousands by identifying
potential issues before you buy. We recommend inspections for all property types and can connect you with qualified,
licensed inspectors in your area.

How long does it take to buy or sell a home?

For buyers, the process typically takes 30-45 days from offer acceptance to closing, though finding the right home
can take weeks or months. For sellers, the average time on market varies by location and price range, but our
properties typically sell within 28 days. We'll provide realistic timelines based on your specific situation and local
market conditions.

What makes EliteHomes different from other real estate companies?

We combine cutting-edge technology with personalized service, offering virtual tours, AI-powered property matching,
comprehensive market analytics, and a dedicated team of experts for each client. Our 98% client satisfaction rate and
average 21-day selling time speak to our commitment to excellence and results.
